Simple questions. Useful next steps.

Farmers often speak in Telugu and English together. This project lets them ask in the words they use every day, then find guidance about crop health, fertilizer, water, prices, weather, and government support.

Type a question on the home page, review the suggested topic and crop, then open the full guide. Change the crop or calculator inputs to match your own field.

Crop symptoms can have more than one cause. The guides help you prepare for a conversation with your agriculture officer; they do not replace a field visit, a soil test, or official scheme verification.

Your questions stay with you

The last five questions are kept in this browser when browser storage is available. Use Clear history on the home page to remove them. Please avoid entering personal identity or bank information.

Weather requests share the chosen city coordinates with Open-Meteo. Custom city searches share the city name with its location service. Voice input is optional and your browser’s speech service may process audio online. Check your browser settings before using it.
