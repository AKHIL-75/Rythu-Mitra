# Answer and uncertainty policy

- The maintained crop catalog and slots.json provide extraction aliases. Bare pasupu is a colour; another named crop wins. Explicit turmeric without colour context is a crop. A leaf colour never proves a disease or a nutrient shortage.
- Named one-word topics such as weather, price and scheme use keyword fallback and ask for missing district/crop details. Unrelated text is asked to become a farming question. A crop name alone or low-confidence model-only guess leads to topic tap buttons, not a confident unrelated answer.
- Symptoms retrieve existing knowledge-base causes/care. No matching verified entry means a plain unavailability statement, agriculture-officer referral and 1800-180-1551. Model problem names are optional low-risk wording suggestions above 0.55; they are not diagnoses. Probability fields and top-three results refer to the raw intent model, not the deterministic fallback label.
- Growth stage and district are contextual slots. They do not establish fertilizer dose, disease cause or scheme eligibility. Guide parameters prefill the existing controls and show results automatically.
- Historical MSP has a dated source. Live mandi retrieval is optional, key-backed, cached for ten minutes and retains the reporting date/market. Missing key or failure produces no invented rate. Fixtures used in API/browser tests are explicitly synthetic test inputs, never reported as observed market data.
- The speak button and microphone activation are removed. The requested simulated voice errors are tested through a compatibility message renderer, with no microphone access. Real-phone microphone testing is not applicable unless speech is deliberately restored later.
- The v2 challenge is authored synthetic language, not a real farmer sample. One repeated v1 query was excluded. Supplied regression examples are present; rule scores are therefore not independent deployment validation. Tests/benchmarks never enter model fitting or validation selection.
- Problem-model scores are conditioned on the correct intent. The 49-query challenge uses explicit problem names and covers only part of the label set. The weak cross-script score is retained and must not be hidden behind the easy workbook score.
